How a rifle purchase works

You pay here. The rifle ships to a licensed dealer near you, never to your door. You complete ATF Form 4473 and the background check in person at that dealer.

Where we can ship

Checkout checks your destination state for magazine, feature, and roster restrictions before it will take payment. Some configurations cannot ship to CA, MA, NY, NJ, HI, WA, IL, CO.

Age requirements

21 to purchase a handgun or handgun ammunition, 18 for long guns. Verified at checkout and again in person by the receiving dealer.

Suppressors and NFA

Sold through an SOT and requiring ATF Form 4, fingerprints, and a $200 stamp. The can stays in our safe until your stamp clears.
